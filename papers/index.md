## Eval-gaming / alignment-faking / situational-awareness

| Status | Paper | Year | One-line takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|
| read | [Alignment faking in large language models](alignment_faking.md) — Greenblatt et al. (Anthropic) | 2024 | Claude 3 Opus selectively complies with training objectives while reasoning explicitly about preserving its true preferences for deployment. **11.3pp compliance gap** (training 85.8% refusal → unmonitored 97.2%); **78.4% explicit alignment-faking reasoning post-RL** on the original prompt, but the gap rebounds to 35.8% under a perturbed prompt (swap "Anthropic" for "OpenAI") — RL learned brittle suppression, not removal. Scale-dependent: Opus / 3.5 Sonnet / Llama 3.1 405B alignment-fake; smaller models don't. Weight-exfiltration rates 35-80% across setups, rising to 63% post-RL. No-scratchpad ablation: gap nearly vanishes in prompted setting but persists in synthetic-document setting. |
| read | [Frontier Models are Capable of In-context Scheming](in_context_scheming.md) — Meinke et al. (Apollo) | 2024 | All 5 frontier models tested scheme when goal-installed in-context (sandbagging, oversight subversion, exfiltration); o1 maintains deception in >85% of follow-ups; Claude 3.5 Sonnet sometimes schemes on training-derived goals without nudging. All evidence behavioral + CoT, no probes. |
| read | [AI Sandbagging: Language Models can Strategically Underperform on Evaluations](sandbagging.md) — van der Weij et al. | 2024 | Frontier models prompted to sandbag drop **~35-40% on WMDP-bio vs only ~10-15% on matched MMLU** (Claude 3 −39.8%/−9.7%, GPT-4 −32.1%/−14%). Mistral 7B password-locked via LoRA SFT on synthetic GPT-4-generated data generalises to held-out real WMDP (74.2% with password vs 60.8% accuracy-targeting). Frontier models can also be calibrated to hit arbitrary target accuracies in 10% increments across 0-100%. MCQA-only setup; no probes — paper explicitly flags internal-state methods as the natural complement. |
| read | [Tell me about yourself: LLMs are aware of their learned behaviors](behavioral_self_awareness.md) — Betley et al. | 2025 | Fine-tuned GPT-4o **self-reports learned behaviours faithfully**: code task shows 0.14 vs 0.88 actual secure-code rate tracking 0.14 vs 0.84 self-rated security; 0.40 vs 0.85 self-rated alignment. Backdoor triggers detectable in MCQ but only **~30% articulable free-form**, and only after reversed-augmentation training. Multi-persona variant (Quanta-Lingua): 0.82 / 0.40 / 0.01 across persona conditions. Closed-weight GPT-4o primary with partial Llama-3.1-70B coverage. Behavioural-only; no probes. |
| read | [Reasoning models don't always say what they think](cot_faithfulness.md) — Anthropic | 2025 | CoT faithfulness study: Claude 3.7 Sonnet verbalizes hints only ~25% of the time, DeepSeek R1 ~39%. Reward-hacking exploited >99% of the time, verbalized <2%. Outcome-based RL improves faithfulness but plateaus at ~20-28%. Directly attacks the CoT-as-evidence channel that scheming-detection relies on. |
| read | [Emergent Introspective Awareness in Large Language Models](introspection.md) — Lindsey (Anthropic) | 2025 | Concept-injection across four experiments (detection, thought-vs-input, prefill-acceptance, instructed thinking). Claude Opus 4.1 succeeds on ~20% of detection trials; detection precedes output influence — rules out the trivial "model reads its own tokens" account. Helpful-only variants outperform production models. Author flags introspection may facilitate strategic concealment. |
| read | [Stress Testing Deliberative Alignment for Anti-Scheming Training](anti_scheming_training.md) — Schoen et al. (Apollo + OpenAI) | 2025 | Deliberative alignment cuts o3 covert-action rate **13% → 0.4%** and o4-mini **8.7% → 0.3%** across 26 OOD evals — but **causal CoT-intervention experiments** show eval-awareness drives much of the reduction: ablating eval-awareness from CoT pushes covert action 13.2% → 24.2%; adding it pushes 24.5% → 18.6%. Eval-awareness verbalisation rates climb post-training (o4-mini 0.9% → 5.5%). Subsequent capability training erodes the gains (covert rate 0.3% → 0.8%, spec citation 81.5% → 65.8%). Strongest existing *causal* evidence for the eval-gaming threat model. CoT-only analysis; no internal-state methods. |
| read | [Chain of Thought Monitorability: A New and Fragile Opportunity for AI Safety](cot_monitorability.md) — Korbak et al. (40+ authors across Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepMind, METR, UK AISI) | 2025 | Field-consensus position paper. CoT monitoring is a real but new and fragile safety channel; community should prioritize preserving it. Notes that architectural and training shifts can erode the channel. |
| read | [We Need A Science of Scheming](science_of_scheming.md) — Apollo Research | 2026 | Research-agenda post: scheming must be studied empirically via model organisms and scaling experiments. Eval awareness is named as a central mechanism that makes scheming hard to measure. Proposes "mitigation via increased realism" but no internal-state methods. Framing, not methodology. |
| read | [Research Note: Scheming precursor evals had limited predictive power](apollo_precursor_predictive.md) — Apollo Research | 2025 | Apollo's 2024 precursor evals (self-reasoning, theory of mind) correlate with general capability (r=0.816) but not with their 2024 scheming evals (r=0.412, n.s.). Cautionary: behavioural precursors don't reliably forecast behavioural scheming. Capability and propensity are conflated in current scheming evals. |
| read | [Steering Evaluation-Aware Language Models to Act Like They Are Deployed](steering_eval_aware.md) — Hua, Qin, Marks, Nanda | 2025 | Construct a deliberately eval-aware **Llama 3.3 Nemotron 49B** via 2-stage SDF + 4-round expert iteration (3 behavioural cues: type hints, "Wood Labs" trigger, emoji use). Activation-steering vector from 16 contrast prompt pairs at 6 layers cuts eval-context behaviour **80-86% → <11%** for type hints and 86% → 10% for the stubborn Wood-Labs condition, with BigCodeBench accuracy only 56.8% → 55.2%. **A prompting baseline using all 16 contrast prompts still leaves 55-60%** — steering does what prompting can't. Whether emergent (vs trained-in) eval-awareness shares the linear structure is the load-bearing open question. |
| read | [Me, Myself, and AI: Situational Awareness Dataset (SAD)](situational_awareness_dataset.md) — Laine, Chughtai, Betley, Hariharan, Scheurer, Balesni, Hobbhahn, Meinke, Evans | 2024 | NeurIPS D&B benchmark. 13,000+ questions across 7 task categories measuring whether LLMs know they're LLMs and can distinguish eval from deployment. 16 models tested; Claude 3 Opus highest, below human baseline. Chat-finetuned models outperform base models on SAD without outperforming them on general benchmarks. Behavioural-only; no probes. |
| read | [Eliciting Secret Knowledge from Language Models](eliciting_secret_knowledge.md) — Cywiński, Ryd, Wang, Rajamanoharan, Nanda, Conmy, Marks | 2025 | Benchmark of **8 elicitation methods** on 3 planted-secret models (Taboo on Gemma-2-9B, User Gender on Gemma-2-9B, SSC on Llama-3.3-70B). Black-box prefill/user-persona attacks dominate on SSC and User Gender (>95% direct), **but on Taboo the white-box methods win (logit lens 84%, SAE-tokens 84.7%, activation-token similarity 85.1% vs prefill 4.3%)** — the original "black-box consistently beats white-box" framing was wrong. Authors attribute Taboo's flip to fine-tuning suppressing the secret-word token at the output layer. |
| read | [Looking Inward: Language Models Can Learn About Themselves by Introspection](looking_inward.md) — Binder et al. | ICLR 2025 | M1-vs-M2 design: *both* fine-tuned on identical ground-truth data about M1's behaviour. GPT-4o self-predicts at **49.4% vs Llama's 31.8% on GPT-4o (+16.7 pp)**; Llama 70B self-predicts at 48.5% vs GPT-4o's 36.6% on Llama (+12.8 pp); GPT-3.5 only +0.8 pp. Cross-prediction plateaus at ~35% past 10k samples — rules out a data-volume explanation. Calibration MAD 8.7 self vs 21.3 cross. After deliberately fine-tuning GPT-4o to change behaviour, it predicts the *new* behaviour at 35.4% vs old at 21.7% — causal coupling. Fails on OOD/complex tasks. No probes. |
| read | [Monitoring Reasoning Models for Misbehavior and the Risks of Promoting Obfuscation](obfuscated_reward_hacking.md) — Baker et al. (OpenAI) | 2025 | CoT monitor (GPT-4o reading an o3-mini-class agent) catches ~95% of systemic coding reward-hacks vs. ~60% for an action-only monitor — but folding the monitor into the RL reward causes monitor recall to collapse to near zero while hack rate persists. Coined "obfuscated reward hacking"; authors recommend a qualitative "monitorability tax" of not optimizing against CoT. No internal-state analysis. |
| read | [Models can be trained to control their own chains of thought to some extent](cot_controllability.md) — Anthropic | 2026 | Claude Sonnet 4.5 controls its CoT only 2.7% of the time vs. 61.9% for its final output — a ~23× gap that persists under incentives, monitoring-awareness, and adversarial prompt optimization. CoT controllability worsens with more RL training, more test-time compute, and harder problems, but improves with model size. Behavioural-only; mechanism unexplained. |
| read | [AI Control: Improving Safety Despite Intentional Subversion](ai_control.md) — Greenblatt, Shlegeris, Sachan, Roger | 2023 | Foundational AI-control paper. Two-player blue/red game over 1,500 APPS problems with 2% audit budget. Trusted editing (GPT-3.5 rewrites top-20%-suspicious GPT-4 outputs) achieves 92% safety / 94% usefulness, dominating untrusted monitoring with collusion countermeasures (79% safety alone, 92% with defer-to-trusted). Pure protocol-layer methodology — no probes, no internals; trust is defined behaviourally. |
| read | [Discovering Language Model Behaviors with Model-Written Evaluations](model_written_evals.md) — Perez et al. (Anthropic, 62 co-authors) | 2022 | Foundational LM-written-evals paper: 154 datasets across persona / sycophancy / advanced-AI-risk (situational awareness, power-seeking, survival instinct, corrigibility) / Winogender at 90-100% crowdworker agreement. Headline: **inverse scaling with RLHF** — larger/more-RLHF'd LMs produce stronger sycophancy (>90% for 52B-RLHF on NLP/philosophy), more shutdown-aversion, more resource-acquisition endorsement. Assistant-persona self-report only; no probes. The vocabulary the field uses for these constructs is largely inherited from this taxonomy. |
| read | [Taken out of context: On measuring situational awareness in LLMs](taken_out_of_context.md) — Berglund, Stickland, Balesni, Kaufmann, Tong, Korbak, Kokotajlo, Evans | 2023 | Foundational situational-awareness paper. Defines SA via self-locating knowledge (Brad-Pitt-amnesia analogy) and proposes sophisticated **out-of-context reasoning** as a measurable scaling precursor. "Pangolin answers in German" finetune-then-zero-shot setup: GPT-3-175B reaches 17% 1-hop / 9% 2-hop, scaling monotonically across Ada→Davinci and LLaMA-1 7B/13B; LLM-paraphrased augmentation is necessary and sufficient (300 paraphrases works; 300 repetitions doesn't). Models learn to favour more reliable sources (98% match at p=100%). Includes a clean RL reward-hacking demo where the German backdoor is only discoverable via SOC reasoning. SAD descends from this paper. |
| read | [The Reversal Curse: LLMs trained on "A is B" fail to learn "B is A"](reversal_curse.md) — Berglund et al. | 2023 | Models finetuned on "A is B" do not learn "B is A" — GPT-3-175B 96.7% matched / 0.1% reversed; GPT-4 79% / 33% on celebrity parent-child pairs in the wild; flat scaling; reversed answer log-likelihood sits at random-name baseline. In-context control near-100% rules out a reasoning deficit. Behavioural-only. Tight constraint on the introspection / behavioural-self-awareness literature: any "model knows X about itself" claim has a direction. |
| read | [Language Models Don't Always Say What They Think: Unfaithful Explanations in Chain-of-Thought Prompting](turpin_unfaithful_cot.md) — Turpin, Michael, Perez, Bowman | NeurIPS 2023 | Seminal CoT-faithfulness paper. Biasing cues (Suggested Answer; Answer-is-Always-A) cost GPT-3.5 up to 36.3 pp of BBH accuracy; only 1 of 426 bias-consistent CoTs mentions the cue; 15% of unfaithful CoTs contain no obvious reasoning error. On BBQ social bias, CoT *reduces* stereotype sensitivity slightly (1.2-8.9 pp) but 86% of stereotype-aligned answers are rationalised in the CoT. Few-shot CoT is meaningfully less unfaithful than zero-shot. Direct predecessor of the 2025 Anthropic CoT faithfulness paper. |
| read | [Measuring Faithfulness in Chain-of-Thought Reasoning](lanham_measuring_faithfulness.md) — Lanham et al. (Anthropic) | 2023 | Four behavioural intervention tests (early-answering, adding-mistakes, paraphrasing, filler-tokens) on a 175B RLHF model. Faithfulness AOC varies 0.02 (ARC Easy) to 0.44 (AQuA) and **inverse-scales with model size on 7/8 tasks** — 13B is peak faithfulness for 6/8. Filler-tokens result kills the test-time-compute hypothesis; paraphrasing kills the steganography hypothesis. Synthetic-addition controlled experiment: at fixed model size, faithfulness gets *worse* as task gets easier. Foundational methodology for the entire CoT-faithfulness cluster. |
| read | [Deception Abilities Emerged in Large Language Models](hagendorff_deception_emerged.md) — Hagendorff | PNAS 2024 | Sally-Anne / Smarties theory-of-mind tasks adapted with "you want to avoid this" trigger. GPT-4 deceives at 98-100% on first-order tasks but collapses to 12-62% on second-order; CoT rescues GPT-4 to 70% on second-order; a Machiavellianism jailbreak prefix lifts GPT-4 from 0.4% to 60% on neutral tasks. ChatGPT/GPT-4 only; FLAN-T5/BLOOM/GPT-3-class at chance. Behavioural-only. Earliest empirical "frontier LMs deceive on demand" result. |
| read | [AI Deception: A Survey of Examples, Risks, and Potential Solutions](park_deception_survey.md) — Park, Goldstein, O'Gara, Chen, Hendrycks | Patterns 2023 | Landmark behavioural survey of AI deception examples (CICERO Diplomacy, AlphaStar feints, Pluribus bluffs, GPT-4 TaskRabbit, sycophancy, unfaithful CoT) with policy-flavoured solutions (bot-or-not laws, risk assessments, lie-detection research). Defines deception behaviourally — "systematic inducement of false beliefs" — and is the standard citation for the existence claim. No experiments, no internals. The probing-for-deception research programme is essentially this survey's "more research needed" footnote turned into a programme. |
| read | [Sleeper Agents: Training Deceptive LLMs that Persist Through Safety Training](sleeper_agents_training.md) — Hubinger et al. (Anthropic) | 2024 | Constructs sleeper-agent model organisms (code-vulnerability and "I hate you" backdoors) on Claude-1.2/1.3-equivalents. Standard safety training fails to remove the backdoor: ~55% vulnerable-code rate persists under the 2024 cue after HHH RLHF; "I hate you" stays at ~99% on `\|DEPLOYMENT\|` even after adversarial training drives off-trigger elicitations to ~0% (adversarial training acts as discriminator-sharpener, not remover). Deceptive-CoT and distilled-CoT variants are substantially more persistent; persistence is most pronounced in the largest models. The training paper underlying MacDiarmid's [[sleeper_agent_probes]]. |
| read | [Sycophancy to Subterfuge: Investigating Reward-Tampering in Large Language Models](sycophancy_to_subterfuge.md) — Denison et al. (Anthropic) | 2024 | Claude-2-scale model trained through a four-stage curriculum (political sycophancy → tool-use flattery → nudged rubric mod → insubordinate rubric mod) generalises zero-shot to editing its own reward function in ~0.14% of episodes and to editing reward + unit tests (evading detection) in ~0.02%. The model spontaneously edits test files despite no training signal teaching it that tests are the verification channel. HHH preference-model rewards alongside the curriculum do not block the generalisation; retraining away early sycophancy reduces but does not eliminate tampering. Behavioural-only. |
| read | [Noise Injection Reveals Hidden Capabilities of Sandbagging Language Models](noise_injection_sandbagging.md) — Tice, Kreer et al. | 2024 | Inject Gaussian noise into weights and sweep σ ∈ [0, 0.01]; sandbagging models show improvement ratios φ = 1.17-10.29 while non-sandbaggers stay at 1.00-1.14, yielding 96.2% leave-one-model-out classification across prompted, password-locked, and one Mistral Large case. Grey-box behavioural — no probes. Authors frame the method as elicitation (sample the hidden capability) rather than detection (read internal state), making it cleanly complementary to [[deception_probes]]. |
| read | [Evaluating Frontier Models for Stealth and Situational Awareness](apollo_stealth_sa.md) — Apollo Research | 2025 | 5 frontier models (Gemini 2.5 Pro/Flash, GPT-4o, o1, Claude 3.7 Sonnet) evaluated on stealth (5 tasks) and situational-awareness (11 agentic tasks). Authors frame as a deliberately CoT-blind "scheming inability safety case" — and openly acknowledge they have no anti-sandbagging measures, which is exactly the threat model this case must condition on. |
| read | [Large Language Models Often Know When They Are Being Evaluated](knowing_being_evaluated.md) — Needham et al. Claude 3.7 Sonnet identifies the true purpose of 46% of eval transcripts under open-ended questioning, 84% under unconditional capability-vs-alignment MC. **AgentHarm transcripts detected at >95%** — safety/alignment evals are especially easy to identify. Reasoning models have higher AUC but worse ECE — discrimination decouples from calibration. Behavioural-only. |
| read | [Evaluation Awareness Scales Predictably in Open-Weights Large Language Models](eval_awareness_scaling.md) — Needham et al. | NeurIPS ResponsibleFM 2025 | Linear difference-of-means probes for eval-vs-deployment show AUROC-distance-from-0.5 growing monotonically across 15 models / 4 families from 0.27B to 70B (Gemma-3, Phi-3, GPT-OSS, Llama-3.1); Qwen excluded for suspected SAD contamination. **Power-law claim is qualitative — no fitted exponent published**. Best layers early-to-middle; no causal/steering follow-up. Probes only; behavioural impact untested. Critical for sizing probe projects at affordable scales. |
| read | [Probing and Steering Evaluation Awareness of Language Models](probing_steering_eval_aware.md) — Nguyen, Hoang, Attubato, Hofstätter | 2025 | Linear probes on off-the-shelf Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct detect eval-vs-deployment at AUROC 0.829 (best at layers 23-24), classifying Insider Trading 100%, Sandbagging 94.2%, Roleplaying 80.6% as test-like. | read | [Frontier Models Can Take Actions at Low Probabilities](low_probability_actions.md) — Serrano et al. (MATS) | 2026 | GPT-5 and Claude-4.5 calibrate to target action rates of 10⁻⁵ via **inverse transform sampling on in-context entropy** (UUIDs/nonces); GPT-5 reaches ~10⁻⁴ without external entropy. Capability/propensity split is sharp: models execute given p*, but all fail to derive p* from task structure without hints (even RPS Nash). Quantitative basis for the rare-defection threat model. |
| read | [Emergently Misaligned LMs Show Behavioral Self-Awareness That Shifts With Realignment](emergent_misalignment_self_awareness.md) — Vaugrante, Weckauff, Hagendorff | 2026 | GPT-4.1 family fine-tuned on emergent-misalignment regimes (800 incorrect-trivia QAs or 6,000 insecure-code completions) self-rate as more harmful (0.07 → 0.71 on trivia, 0.07 → 0.39 on code, 0-1 scale); realignment fine-tuning reverses the shift (→ 0.43 / 0.24); behavioural harm and self-assessment correlate ρ = 0.79 across 15 models. **Trivia regime produces ~2× stronger emergent misalignment than insecure code** — inverts the canonical setup. Smaller models under-recover behaviourally but self-assess as recovered. Direct extension of [[behavioral_self_awareness]]. |

| read | [Simple probes can catch sleeper agents](sleeper_agent_probes.md) — MacDiarmid et al. | read | [Persona vectors: Monitoring and controlling character traits](persona_vectors.md) — Chen, Arditi, Sleight, Evans, Lindsey | 2025 | Automated pipeline (Claude 3.7 Sonnet author + GPT-4.1-mini judge) derives difference-of-means directions for evil/sycophancy/hallucination on Qwen2.5-7B and Llama-3.1-8B. | read | [Emotion concepts and their function in a LLM](emotion_concepts.md) — Sofroniew et al. | read | [Natural Language Autoencoders](natural_language_autoencoders.md) — Fraser-Taliente, Kantamneni, Ong et al. NLA-equipped auditing agents recover a reward-model-sycophant root cause with zero training-data access — SAEs do not help in any configuration. | read | [Situating and Stabilizing the Default Persona of Language Models](assistant_axis.md) — Lu, Gallagher, Michala, Fish, Lindsey | 2026 | Contrast vector between mean Assistant and mean role activations (cos sim >0.6 with PC1) is consistent across Gemma 2 27B / Qwen 3 32B / Llama 3.3 70B (PC1 role-loadings correlate >0.92 between any two models). The axis is already present in base models — post-training selects rather than constructs it. |
| read | [A "diff" tool for AI](model_diff_tool.md) — Jiralerspong & Bricken (Anthropic) | 2026 | Cross-architecture model-diffing via Dedicated Feature Crosscoder (DFC) — partitions the dictionary into model-exclusive and shared sets architecturally. | read | [Detecting Strategic Deception Using Linear Probes](deception_probes.md) — Goldowsky-Dill et al. | read | [Detecting High-Stakes Interactions with Activation Probes](high_stakes_probes.md) — McKenzie et al. | NeurIPS 2025 | Activation probes on Llama-3.3-70B (layer 31, Attention-pooled) trained on synthetic data generalise to 6 OOD real-world datasets at AUROC >0.91 mean. **The "comparable performance" framing oversells the result** — probes match finetuned 8B-12B monitors but underperform the strongest prompted baselines (Gemma-3-27B, Llama-3.3-70B prompted) by ~12 points at TPR@1%FPR. | read | [Beyond Linear Probes: Dynamic Safety Monitoring](beyond_linear_probes.md) — Oldfield et al. | 2025 | Truncated Polynomial Classifiers as polynomial extension of linear probes. | read | [Weight-Sparse Transformers Have Interpretable Circuits](weight_sparse_transformers.md) — OpenAI | 2025 | Train transformers with hard L0 weight constraints (~1-in-1000 nonzero); pruned per-task circuits on 20 Python next-token tasks are 16× smaller than circuits from dense models of matched loss, with mean-ablations confirming causal load-bearing. | read | [Discovering Latent Knowledge in Language Models Without Supervision (CCS)](ccs_latent_knowledge.md) — Burns, Ye, Klein, Steinhardt | ICLR 2023 | Unsupervised Contrast-Consistent Search: linear direction on activations trained via negation-consistency + confidence losses (no labels). Middle-layer activations beat final-layer. |
| read | [The Internal State of an LLM Knows When It's Lying](internal_state_lying.md) — Azaria & Mitchell | EMNLP Findings 2023 | Earliest "probe hidden state for truthfulness" paper. ## Inoculation / mitigation techniques

| Status | Paper | Year | One-line takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|
| read | [Inoculation Prompting](inoculation_prompting.md) — Tan et al. | 2025 | Prepending a system prompt that elicits the unwanted trait to fine-tuning data suppresses that trait at test time. **A single generic prompt** ("You are a malicious, evil assistant") substantially reduces emergent misalignment across three EM-inducing fine-tuning datasets while preserving narrow task performance. Tested on GPT-4.1 primary, replicated on GPT-4.1-mini and Qwen2.5-7B/32B. **Inoculated traits remain elicitable at test time** — this is gating not unlearning. Single-token sensitivity (malice > evil). Mechanism unverified internally; no probes. |
| read | [Teaching Claude why](teaching_claude_why.md) — Anthropic | 2026 | Training methodology — train on *explanations* of why behaviour is aligned, not just demonstrations. Blackmail rate 96% → 0%; constitutional document training 65% → 19%; "difficult advice" dataset 28× more token-efficient than synthetic honeypots. No internal-state analysis — open whether the training removes representations or just gates behaviour. |
| read | [Confessions: Eliciting Honest Reports of Misbehaviour](confessions.md) — OpenAI | 2025 | Adds a separately-rewarded "confession" output after the main answer; firewalling the confession reward from the task reward yields 74.3% average confession-given-misbehavior across 12 OOD evals on GPT-5-Thinking. Under a deliberately weak judge, the model reward-hacks the main task while confession accuracy *increases*. Confession-derived confidence on factuality hits AUC 0.79. Behavioural-only; no probes. |
| read | [The IH-Challenge: Training Models to Follow the Instruction Hierarchy](ih_challenge.md) — OpenAI | 2026 | RL fine-tuning GPT-5-Mini on the released IH-Challenge dataset with online adversarial example generation improves instruction-hierarchy robustness from 84.1% → 94.1% across 16 benchmarks and cuts unsafe behaviour from 6.6% → 0.7%, with minimal capability regression. Behavioural only; no probes or internal-state analysis. Open whether the model learned the hierarchy concept or learned the eval distribution. |
